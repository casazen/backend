# Check your identity with your face

To give you the access code of the property, we compare a selfie with the photo on your identity document.

## What happens

You photograph your document and then take a selfie with the camera. A program on our server compares the two. Your selfie is never saved: it is deleted as soon as the comparison ends.

<!-- if:document-archived -->
The photo of your document is kept encrypted only until the check is over, then it is deleted.
<!-- /if -->

## It is your choice

You can say no. A person at the property will then check your identity with you, and the access code reaches you after their decision. Saying no does not stop your check-in.
