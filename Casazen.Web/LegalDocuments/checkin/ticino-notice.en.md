# Check-in in Ticino: how we process your data

The property is in Switzerland, in the canton of Ticino. This notice explains how we process your data in the check-in and in the identity verification, under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP, in force since 1 September 2023).

## Who is the controller

The controller is the agency that manages the property. CasaZen provides the platform to the agency and processes the data on its behalf. The contact details of the agency are in your booking confirmation.

## What we process

- the data you write in the check-in: name, date of birth, citizenship and the details of your document;
- the photo of your identity document and a selfie, only if you agree to the face check.

## Your document is not archived

For a property in Ticino we do not keep the photo of your document. We use it only to read the data and to compare it with the selfie, then it does not stay on our server. The check-in data stay on record for the legal duties to register guests.

## The photos are deleted at once

A program on our server compares the selfie with the photo of the document. The two photos serve only for this comparison and are deleted as soon as it ends: we do not save them and do not use them for anything else.

## The face check is optional

You can say no. A person at the property will then check your identity with you and no photo is compared.

## Your rights

You can ask the agency which data it processes about you, to correct them and, where the law allows it, to delete them or to restrict their use. If you think your data are not processed correctly you can contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

## Contact

To exercise your rights write to the agency that manages the property, at the address in your booking confirmation.
