7. Security measures (art. 32 GDPR)

7.1 CasaZen applies the following technical and organizational measures, implemented in the Service on the date the DPA comes into force:

  • Separation of data by Organization. Every item of data is associated with the Controller's Organization; on every read the Service automatically applies the Organization filter, and a resource of another Organization appears as not found.
  • Authentication and role-based access. Every request is authenticated with a verified token and authorized by rules per role and per individual resource; platform administration functions are reserved to a dedicated role; an account can be deactivated.
  • Encryption in transit. The Service is accessed over HTTPS, with HSTS on the APIs; the connection to the database requires TLS.
  • Column-level encryption of the most sensitive data. Number and place of issue of the documents of guests and of members of the group, Alloggiati Web credentials, keys for booking portals and calendar import addresses are encrypted in the database with authenticated encryption (AES-256-CBC with HMAC-SHA256); the keys are stored encrypted with a certificate of the environment and rotate automatically. Name, email and phone number are not encrypted at column level because they are used for searches; they are protected by access control and by the measures of the infrastructure providers.
  • Private storage of documents. Copies of documents, contracts and receipts are in a non-public storage, readable only through an authenticated API with an Organization check or through signed links lasting a few minutes.
  • Reduced exposure of data. The document number is shown masked (last three characters) and in clear only on explicit, logged request; Alloggiati Web credentials are never returned by the APIs.
  • Links and tokens. Check-in and confirmation links are random, have an expiry and are stored only as a fingerprint (hash); the calendar export links contain no guest data.
  • Payments. Card numbers do not pass through CasaZen; Stripe notifications are accepted only with a verified signature and processed once only.
  • Technical logs without personal data in clear. Application logs do not contain email addresses in clear (they are masked), names, access tokens or credentials.
  • Protection of interfaces. HTTP security headers, an allow-list of origins without credentials, rate limiting of public functions, and checks against access to internal addresses when the Service downloads addresses provided by users.
  • Traceability. Exports, erasures, anonymizations and applications of retention periods are recorded without the guest's personal data; the consent history is kept in append-only form.
  • Secrets and configuration. The Service's keys and credentials are in the variables of the execution environments, not in the code; in production the application does not start without the required encryption configuration.
  • Deletion and minimization. Tools for erasure, anonymization and export per guest, and a nightly process that applies the configured retention periods (art. 13).
  • Backups. Managed by the database provider, according to its retention times.

7.2 The Controller is responsible for the correct assignment of roles to its collaborators, the confidentiality of credentials and the security of its own devices.

7.3 CasaZen may update the measures to adapt them to the state of the art, provided it does not reduce the overall level of security.

10. Assistance with data subjects' rights

10.1 If a data subject contacts CasaZen to exercise a right over the Controller Data, CasaZen invites them to contact the Controller and forwards the request without undue delay; it does not respond on the merits without the Controller's instructions, unless required by law.

10.2 CasaZen assists the Controller with appropriate technical and organizational measures, in particular with the Service's tools:

  • access and portability (arts. 15 and 20): export of a guest's data in a structured JSON file (identity, birth, document, bookings and payments, members of the stays, check-in sessions, Alloggiati communications, consent history), with the request GET /api/gdpr/guests/{id}/export; summary of consents and retention with GET /api/gdpr/guests/{id};
  • rectification (art. 16 GDPR): editing of the data of the guest and of the members of the group in the Service's records;
  • erasure (art. 17 GDPR): DELETE /api/gdpr/guests/{id}, which deletes the personal fields of the guest and of the members of their stays, the copy of the document, the IP address and notes of the consents and the special requests of the bookings; it is not allowed while a stay is open, because the communication required by law is still under way (art. 17.3.b GDPR); a repeated request changes nothing;
  • anonymization: POST /api/gdpr/guests/{id}/anonymize, with the same effects without marking the record as deleted;
  • marketing consent and objection (arts. 7.3 and 21): PUT /api/gdpr/guests/{id}/consent; the Controller cannot grant consent in place of the guest and can record its withdrawal only on the guest's documented request, with a note;
  • parties to lease contracts: POST /api/leases/{id}/erasure-request; the parties' data is anonymized after the contract has ended;
  • tax data of the Organization: GET /api/gdpr/org/export and POST /api/gdpr/org/anonymize.

10.3 For requests not covered by these tools (for example restriction of processing, art. 18 GDPR) CasaZen assists on written request. Assistance is provided in time for the Controller to respond within the period of art. 12.3 GDPR.

10.4 What remains after an erasure. What remains: the guest's record with names replaced by "ANONYMIZED" and a placeholder email address, because bookings and communications are linked to it; bookings with dates, amounts, payments and Stripe references, which are the Controller's accounting records; the consent events (purpose, action, version, date), without IP address or notes, as proof of consent or of the notice; the status of the Alloggiati communication and the receipt reference, as proof of a legal obligation; the audit records, which contain no personal data.
