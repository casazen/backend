# Runbook: flussi turistici (Ross1000 Lombardia, ISTAT) e portali regionali

Task CO-22 (issues #6 and #8). Status: **not implemented, by decision**: the public specification could not be verified
from official sources, and D14 / the agent protocol forbid speculative regulatory code. Research: 
`.claude/context/regulations/istat-flussi-turistici.md` (RS-9, 2026-09-24) and `cir-lombardia.md`.

## What is known (official, U) and what is not

- Lombardy requires every ricettiva structure and tourist rental to report the monthly flows on Ross1000
  (`https://www.flussituristici.servizirl.it/`), by day 5 of the next month (L.R. 27/2015 art. 38 c. 8, art. 40 c. 9).
- The data are **per guest, per day** (not an ISTAT aggregate): the Regione produces MOV/C for ISTAT. There is no "ISTAT
  format" for the host.
- Not verified (only third-party sources T/D in RS-9): the exact XML/TXT record layout (mandatory fields, lengths, codes),
  the SOAP `checkinV2` web service (endpoint, operation, authentication, response), the Lombardy test environment.
  The official documents are behind domains blocked by the session network (`flussituristici.servizirl.it`,
  `regione.lombardia.it`, `istat.it`): confirmed again on 2026-10-02 (`EGRESS_BLOCKED`). Not worked around.
