# Demo environment

Staff-only, and only outside Production. In Production `GET /api/admin/demo` reports `available: false` and the other calls answer 404 `demo_unavailable`.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Amministrazione → Altro → Ambiente demo** (`/app/admin/demo`).
2. Turn **Modalità demo** on.
3. Choose the organization.

## Publish the listings that are already there

**Completa conformità di test** writes, on every short-stay property except the showcase draft:

- a CIN in the official format whose random part contains `TEST` and whose ISTAT comune matches the property (Como `013075`, Milano `015146`, Roma `058091`, or the comune already chosen);
- a real one-page PDF for each document the region requires (`CinCertificate`, and `Ape` in Lombardy, `PropertyLicense` in Lazio), stored as a private file named `TEST-….pdf`;
- a safety checklist the activation accepts (no gas, one extinguisher, BDSR declaration, confirmation).

`Properties.IsTestData` and `PropertyDocuments.IsTestData` are set, and a row in `TestDataMarkers` (`origin = compliance`) records each invented row. The property is then activated, so the public site can list it. Payments still depend on Stripe: this does not invent a connected account.

- the activation of CasaZen with Octorate (scenario `onboarding-activation`, E10/F01/US01/T04, NF-104, NF-106): three unpublished short-stay houses (`attivazione-attiva` in Como, `attivazione-da-attivare` in Milano, `attivazione-da-collegare` in Roma), a **connected test connection** of the environment to Octorate (account `TEST-DEMO-OCTORATE`, no token), the listings of the first two confirmed on it to invented accommodations (`TEST-ACC-…`, no product, so the booking and calendar jobs never read them), and the bookings flow activated on the first. The part «Listings» of the plan is deferred, with its "To do" (`WizardResume`, due three days after the reset). The connection and the listings are written **only when the org has no Octorate connection of its own** in the environment (a real one is never touched; the houses, the activation and the deferred part are written anyway), and the deferred part only when the org has no state of its own for that part. The connection has no token: nothing can be called with it, and with `Features__OctorateConnector` on a job of the connector that tries (the OTA conversations every 10 minutes) finds no token and marks it lost, so the plan shows the lost connection and the hourly sync raises the `ReconnectChannelManager` To do; the next reset makes it connected again. The rows are marked (`TestDataEntities.OctorateConnection`, `OctorateListingMapping`, `PropertyFlowActivation`, `OnboardingPartState`, `Todo`) and deleted by the next reset before the houses, together with the marked rows of the other onboarding tables (`OnboardingPanelStep`, `OnboardingAssistRequest`, `OnboardingPilotMember`, `PropertyOnboardingData`). **A marker says what the showcase wrote, not what the row has become**: a real consent of the host reuses the one connection of the org and the environment (its account and tokens go into the marked row), and a deferral or an exit of the host rewrites the one row of the part. So the reset deletes the test connection, the mappings on it and the deferred part only while they are still the invented ones (the connection: the account `TEST-DEMO-OCTORATE` and no token at all; the part: still tied to the "To do" the showcase raised, or holding no intent), together with an unmarked mapping or cursor on an invented connection (the foreign keys are restrictive); a connection the host made real keeps its tokens and its real mappings (only the invented listings `TEST-ACC-…` go), and a part the host put off again keeps its row and its own "To do": all of them only lose their marker, and from then on the seed finds the org's own connection and part and writes neither again. The screens need `Features__OctorateOnboarding` on.

The optional checkbox soft-deletes other unpublished short-stay listings of that organization that have no booking. It does not delete published host listings.

A showcase listing that has a lease cannot be reset (`demo_reset_blocked`): remove the lease first.

## When a feature is added

Update the catalog, the marker and `DemoShowcaseTests` in the same change. The requirement is in specifica rimossa (storico in git) and `.claude/rules/demo-seed.md`.
